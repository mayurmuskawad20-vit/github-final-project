# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, or identity. We want all contributors to feel safe and valued.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and polite to others.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other contributors learn and improve.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these community guidelines. They may take appropriate and fair action when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

These guidelines apply to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Enforcement

If someone experiences or observes unacceptable behavior, they may report it to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review the situation and take appropriate action, which may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, or temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the nature and seriousness of the behavior when deciding how to respond. They will aim to be fair, consistent, and respectful while protecting the community.
